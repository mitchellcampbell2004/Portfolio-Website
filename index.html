About Me

Before Coding

Looking at my resume, it might be a little confusing how I ended up pursuing a career in computer science.

I originally planned on becoming a teacher, starting my college career by getting a B.A. in History and then briefly pursuing a Master's in Teaching. However, I decided to go in a different direction and eventually found computer science. I've always loved solving problems and creating things, which, combined with an interest in technology and gaming, made C.S. a natural fit.
My first foray into the world of coding was learning HTML and CSS through freeCodeCamp. From there, I enrolled in Ball State University's Master's in Computer Science program, and am currently in my first semester.
Since beginning Ball State's program, which focuses heavily on the use of Python, I have become much more proficient in the language and various software development practices. I've also learned how to use Git/GitHub for version control, and am currently learning R, data analysis, and other areas of computer science.
In terms of projects, I am most notably working on a Resale Analyzer. It will become a combination of a calculator, research tool, and data analysis application. The purpose of its creation is to assist resellers in identifying potentially profitable items and tracking metrics such as profit, ROI, and successful resale patterns. As of now, it utilizes Python and CSV files for data storage, but I plan to eventually incorporate R, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and other technologies as appropriate.
I'm also currently working on this portfolio website to continue cultivating my HTML and CSS skills.
Moving forward, I want to continue developing my skills by working on meaningful projects, progressing in my studies through BSU, and filling knowledge gaps through certifications and additional coursework. I'm excited to pursue my first internship and look forward to building a career where I can use technology to help solve real-world problems and contribute to a team doing meaningful work.
